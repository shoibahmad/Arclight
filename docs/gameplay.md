# How to play

A short guide. Everything here is discoverable in-game, but the cell economy
is worth understanding before your first race rather than after it.

---

## The cell is everything

You have one resource. It is called the cell, it sits bottom-right, and it
pays for all three of these:

| | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Boost** (`Shift`) | ~30/s | Extra thrust, and raises top speed about 19% |
| **Shield** (`Space`) | ~21/s | Absorbs barrier and contact damage entirely |
| **Air brakes** (`Q` / `E`) | ~5/s each | Rotate the craft and scrub speed |

Nothing else refills it. There are no pickups.

## Charging

The **arcline** is the glowing ribbon running through the circuit. Riding it
charges the cell, and the closer you are to its centre the faster it charges.
Charge also scales with how fast you are going, so crawling along it is not a
strategy.

Drift more than about four metres off it and you earn nothing.

**The line moves.** Lap one is the clean racing line. From lap two it is
re-derived with a different wander — never more than the road allows, never
sharper than the craft can follow, but genuinely somewhere else. You cannot
memorise it once and stop looking.

The marker at the bottom of the screen shows which side of the line you are
on and how far off. When it turns cyan you are charging.

## Driving

**Lift before you turn, not during.** Grip grows with speed in this game, so
the craft is most planted when it is going fastest and loosest when you lift
mid-corner. Get your braking done in a straight line and carry the speed.

**Use the air brakes.** They are the difference between a tidy hairpin and a
barrier. Hold the one on the inside of the corner — `Q` for a left, `E` for a
right — and the craft rotates far harder than steering alone allows. They
cost speed and cell, which is the point.

**Barriers cost more than time.** A square hit takes a large bite out of your
integrity and most of your speed. At low integrity the craft is slower and
visibly damaged. Shields prevent the damage entirely but drain fast, so they
are for the hit you can see coming, not for insurance.

## Spending

A rough mental model:

- A full cell is about **3.5 seconds of boost**, or about 5 seconds of shield.
- Riding the line perfectly at racing speed earns roughly **25–30 per second**
  — so boost held on the line is close to break-even, and boost held off it
  drains fast.
- That means: **boost on the line, not off it.** The straight where you are
  tracking well is the cheap place to spend.

The craft differ mainly in how they want you to manage this:

- **Vireo** charges 30% faster — it forgives a sloppy line.
- **Mantis** charges slowly and has the smallest cell. It is the fastest craft
  on the grid and it only works if you are genuinely on the line.
- **Oriole** has the deepest shield reserve and can afford to lean on people.
- **Kestrel** is the one to learn on.

## Modes

**Quick race** — pick a circuit, class and craft. You start at the back of
eight. Three or four laps depending on the circuit.

**Championship** — a cup is a sequence of rounds scoring 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4.
Finishing a cup on the podium unlocks the next one, along with the circuits
and craft it introduces. The **Apex** class unlocks after the Nocturne Cup.

**Time trial** — alone on the circuit, against a ghost of your best lap. The
delta above the timer is live: green means you are ahead of the ghost at this
point on the lap. `R` restarts instantly.

---

## Reading the HUD

- **Top left** — position and lap.
- **Top centre** — current lap time, your best, your last. In time trial, the
  live ghost delta.
- **Top right** — the field, with the gap to the leader.
- **Bottom left** — minimap. You are the white blip.
- **Bottom right** — speed, cell, hull integrity, and whether boost or shield
  is currently drawing.
- **Bottom centre** — the arcline marker.

---

## If it runs badly

**Settings → Render scale** first; it is the strongest single dial.
**Settings → Quality** next — LOW switches off shadows and thins the scenery.

Shadows and bloom iterations are the two most expensive things in the frame.
